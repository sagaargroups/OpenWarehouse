# Appreciation / Recognition Letter — Content Module

> **Module Version:** `v1.0`  
> **Compatible Formats:** `a4-letterhead`  
> **Document Type:** Personal recognition for team members, partners, or collaborators  
> **Department:** Culture / Experience  
> **Philosophy:** Recognition should be specific, personal, and timely. Not a generic "great job" email — a genuine acknowledgment of what someone actually did.
