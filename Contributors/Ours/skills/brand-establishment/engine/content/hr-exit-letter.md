# Exit / Offboarding Letter — Content Module

> **Module Version:** `v1.0`  
> **Compatible Formats:** `a4-letterhead`  
> **Document Type:** Separation letter with dignity  
> **Department:** HR  
> **Philosophy:** People leave. That's normal. This document ensures exits are handled with respect, clarity, and zero ambiguity — not with corporate coldness.

---

This letter confirms the conclusion of your engagement with {{BRAND_NAME}}, effective **{{LAST_WORKING_DATE}}**.

{{EXIT_CONTEXT}}

## Section 5: Access Revocation

The following access will be deactivated on your last working date:

| System | Action |
|---|---|
| Company email | Deactivated |
| Internal tools ({{TOOL_LIST}}) | Access revoked |
| Code repositories | Access removed |
| Cloud storage | Personal files downloadable until {{ACCESS_DEADLINE}} |

> **Your personal files:** You have until {{ACCESS_DEADLINE}} to download any personal files from company systems. After that date, access will be permanently revoked.

## Section 6: Continuing Obligations

The following obligations survive your departure:

1. **Confidentiality:** Per your NDA, confidentiality obligations continue for {{CONFIDENTIALITY_SURVIVAL}} after termination.
2. **Non-Solicitation:** {{NON_SOLICIT_TERMS}} (if applicable).
3. **IP Transfer:** All work product created during engagement remains the property of {{BRAND_NAME}}.

## Section 7: Reference and Support

## Section 8: A Genuine Note

Thank you for your contribution, {{MEMBER_NAME}}. We wish you well.
