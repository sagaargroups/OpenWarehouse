## Section 2: Purpose

The Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship concerning:

> {{PURPOSE_DESCRIPTION}}

In connection with this, each Party may disclose certain confidential and proprietary information to the other.

---

## Section 3: Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any and all information disclosed by either Party, whether orally, in writing, electronically, or by observation, including but not limited to:

- Business plans, strategies, and financial information
- Customer and client lists, pricing, and contractual terms
- Technical data, source code, algorithms, and system architectures
- Marketing plans, creative assets, and brand strategies
- Trade secrets, know-how, and proprietary processes
- Any information marked or designated as "Confidential"

---

## Section 4: Exclusions

Confidential Information does **not** include information that:

1. Is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement
2. Was already known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure, as evidenced by written records
3. Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to Confidential Information
4. Is received from a third party without restriction and without breach of any obligation of confidentiality
5. Is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, or court order, provided the Receiving Party gives prompt notice to the Disclosing Party

---

## Section 5: Obligations of Receiving Party

The Receiving Party agrees to:

1. **Protect** Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care used to protect its own confidential information, but no less than reasonable care.
2. **Restrict access** to Confidential Information to employees, contractors, and agents who have a need to know and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as restrictive as this Agreement.
3. **Not disclose** Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent of the Disclosing Party.
4. **Not use** Confidential Information for any purpose other than the Purpose stated in Section 2.
5. **Not copy or reproduce** Confidential Information except as necessary for the Purpose.

---

## Section 6: Term

- **Effective Date:** {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}
- **Term:** This Agreement remains in effect for **{{NDA_TERM}}** from the Effective Date.
- **Survival:** Confidentiality obligations survive termination for **{{SURVIVAL_PERIOD}}** after expiration or termination.

---

## Section 7: Return or Destruction of Materials

Upon termination or written request:

1. The Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information and any copies thereof.
2. The Receiving Party shall certify in writing that all materials have been returned or destroyed.
3. Exception: Copies retained in automated backup systems or required by law may be retained, subject to continuing confidentiality obligations.

---

## Section 8: No License or Obligation

- Nothing in this Agreement grants any license or rights to the Receiving Party under any patent, copyright, trademark, or trade secret.
- Neither Party is obligated to enter into any further agreement or business relationship.
- Neither Party is obligated to disclose any particular Confidential Information.

---

## Section 9: Remedies

Both Parties acknowledge that breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be inadequate. The Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek **injunctive relief** in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

---

## Section 10: General Provisions

- **Governing Law:** This Agreement is governed by the laws of **{{JURISDICTION}}**.
- **Entire Agreement:** This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties regarding confidentiality.
- **Amendments:** Must be in writing and signed by both Parties.
- **Assignment:** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without prior written consent.
- **Severability:** If any provision is held invalid, the remaining provisions continue in full force.
