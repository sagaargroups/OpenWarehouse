### Our Commitments to You

1. **You own everything.** Domains, repos, source files — all registered in your name.
2. **Scope is written down.** What's included, what's not, and what changes cost.
3. **We say when something is a bad idea.** Even when it's your idea.
4. **No invented proof.** No fake testimonials, no inflated metrics.
