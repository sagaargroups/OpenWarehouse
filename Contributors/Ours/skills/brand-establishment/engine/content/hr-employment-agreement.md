## Section 6: Intellectual Property

### 6.1 Company Work Product
All work product created during engagement and within the scope of duties is the property of the Company.

### 6.2 Pre-Existing IP
Team Member retains ownership of any intellectual property created before this engagement. Such IP must be disclosed in **Exhibit A** attached hereto.

### 6.3 Open Source
Contributions to open-source projects related to Company work require prior written approval.

## Section 7: Confidentiality

Team Member agrees to:
1. Maintain confidentiality of all proprietary information during and after this engagement
2. Not disclose Company data, client information, or trade secrets to third parties
3. Return or destroy all confidential materials upon termination

This obligation survives termination for **{{CONFIDENTIALITY_SURVIVAL}}**.

## Section 8: Non-Competition and Non-Solicitation

- **Non-Compete:** {{NON_COMPETE_TERMS}} (if applicable; state "None" if not enforced)
- **Non-Solicitation:** Team Member agrees not to solicit Company clients or team members for **{{NON_SOLICIT_PERIOD}}** after termination.

> **Transparency note:** We don't believe in trapping people. If you're leaving, we want the transition to be clean and respectful. These terms exist only to protect active client relationships.
