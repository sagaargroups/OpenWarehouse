### The 4 Commitments That Govern This Engagement

1. **You Own Everything:** Domains, hosting accounts, repositories, and source files are registered in your name.
2. **Scope is Written Down:** Exactly what is included, what is not, and what changes cost — agreed here, never negotiated after.
3. **We Say When Something is a Bad Idea:** Including when it's your idea, and including when agreeing would be easier.
4. **No Invented Proof:** Zero stock testimonials, zero fake logos. If an area is empty, it's empty because it's true.
