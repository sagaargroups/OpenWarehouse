# Offer Letter — Content Module

> **Module Version:** `v1.0`  
> **Compatible Formats:** `a4-letterhead`  
> **Document Type:** Employment offer / engagement invitation  
> **Department:** HR  
> **Philosophy:** Humanity-first. This is the first branded document a new team member sees. It should feel personal, clear, and welcoming — not corporate.

## Section 5: What We Believe In

> At {{BRAND_NAME}}, we operate on principles, not policies.

1. **Transparency over hierarchy.** You'll always know why decisions are made.
2. **Ownership over permission.** You own your work, your tools, your output.
3. **Quality over speed.** We'd rather be right than first.
4. **People over process.** Processes serve people, never the other way around.
