## Section 3: Term and Termination

- **Effective Date:** {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}
- **Initial Term:** {{TERM_DURATION}} from the Effective Date
- **Renewal:** This Agreement automatically renews for successive {{RENEWAL_PERIOD}} periods unless either party provides {{NOTICE_PERIOD}} written notice of non-renewal.
- **Termination for Cause:** Either party may terminate this Agreement upon {{CURE_PERIOD}} written notice if the other party materially breaches and fails to cure within that period.
- **Termination for Convenience:** Either party may terminate with {{CONVENIENCE_NOTICE}} written notice.

## Section 4: Compensation and Payment

- **Fee Structure:** As defined in each appended SOW.
- **Payment Terms:** All invoices are due within **{{PAYMENT_DAYS}} days** of receipt.
- **Late Payment:** Overdue invoices accrue interest at **{{LATE_INTEREST_RATE}}%** per month.
- **Currency:** All amounts in **{{CURRENCY}}**.
- **Taxes:** Client is responsible for all applicable taxes beyond the stated fees.

---

## Section 5: Intellectual Property

### 5.1 Client-Owned Deliverables
All work product, source code, designs, and creative assets produced under this Agreement are the **exclusive property of the Client** upon full payment of all associated fees.

### 5.2 Provider Tools and Pre-Existing IP
Provider retains ownership of pre-existing tools, frameworks, and methodologies. Client receives a non-exclusive, perpetual license to use such tools as embedded in deliverables.

### 5.3 Transfer Protocol
Upon final settlement and project closure, Provider shall transfer:
- All source code repositories
- All domain registrations
- All hosting account credentials
- All design source files

> **"All client source repositories and IP transferred upon final settlement."**

## Section 6: Confidentiality

Both parties agree to maintain confidentiality of all proprietary information exchanged during the term of this Agreement and for **{{CONFIDENTIALITY_PERIOD}}** after termination.

Confidential information excludes information that:
- Is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving party
- Was already known to the receiving party prior to disclosure
- Is independently developed without reference to confidential information
- Is disclosed pursuant to a legal obligation

## Section 7: Warranties and Disclaimers

- Provider warrants that services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner.
- Provider warrants it has the right to enter into this Agreement and perform the services.
- **No other warranties, express or implied, are made.**
- Provider does not warrant uninterrupted or error-free operation of deliverables.

---

## Section 8: Limitation of Liability

Provider's total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the **total fees paid by Client in the {{LIABILITY_PERIOD}} preceding the claim.** Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, or punitive damages.

---

## Section 9: Indemnification

Each party agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the other party from claims arising from:
- Breach of this Agreement
- Negligence or willful misconduct
- Violation of applicable laws

---

## Section 10: Dispute Resolution

- **Governing Law:** This Agreement is governed by the laws of **{{JURISDICTION}}**.
- **Dispute Resolution:** Disputes shall first be addressed through good-faith negotiation. If unresolved within **{{NEGOTIATION_PERIOD}}**, disputes shall be submitted to **{{ARBITRATION_BODY}}** for binding arbitration.

---

## Section 11: General Provisions

- **Entire Agreement:** This Agreement, together with all SOWs, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties.
- **Amendments:** Must be in writing and signed by both parties.
- **Assignment:** Neither party may assign this Agreement without prior written consent.
- **Severability:** If any provision is held invalid, the remaining provisions continue in effect.
- **Force Majeure:** Neither party is liable for failure to perform due to circumstances beyond reasonable control.
