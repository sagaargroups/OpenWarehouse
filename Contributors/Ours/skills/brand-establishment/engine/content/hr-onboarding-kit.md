## Section 5: How We Work

### Communication
| Channel | When to Use |
|---|---|
| {{PRIMARY_COMMS}} | Day-to-day work, questions, updates |
| Email | External communication, formal records |
| Video Calls | Scheduled sync meetings only |
| {{ASYNC_TOOL}} | Async updates, documentation, handoffs |

### Our Rules
1. **Default to async.** Write it down before booking a call.
2. **Over-communicate, never under.** If in doubt, share.
3. **Block your deep work.** Protect uninterrupted time.
4. **Camera optional.** Presence is measured by output, not video.

---

## Section 6: Our Culture

### What We Believe

- **Transparency over hierarchy.** Decisions are visible. Reasons are stated.
- **Ownership over permission.** You own your domain. Act, then inform.
- **Quality over speed.** Ship right, not fast.
- **People over process.** If a process hurts people, the process is wrong.
- **Feedback is a gift.** Give it direct, receive it openly.

### What We Don't Tolerate
- Gatekeeping information
- Taking credit for others' work
- Passive-aggressive communication
- Performative busyness
