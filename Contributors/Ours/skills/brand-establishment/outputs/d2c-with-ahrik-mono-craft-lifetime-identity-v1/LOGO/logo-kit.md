## 2. Visual Geometry & Design Rationale

- **Wordmark Typography:** Set in bespoke craft serif typography matching `Gloock`, characterized by sharp bracketed serifs, vertical stress, and compact horizontal tracking (`-0.01em`).
- **Contrast Philosophy:** 100% black on white (`#111312` on `#FFFFFF`) delivers maximum visual authority without distracting gradient gimmicks.
- **Craft Spring Integration:** When animated, logo reveals use the brand's signature spring curve `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)`.

## 3. Logo Usage & Clear Space Rules

```text
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                       CLEAR SPACE [X]                       │
│                                                             │
│   [X]    ┌───────────────────────────────────────┐    [X]   │
│          │             D2C With AHrik            │          │
│   [X]    └───────────────────────────────────────┘    [X]   │
│                                                             │
│                       CLEAR SPACE [X]                       │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Clear Space:** Maintain a minimum exclusion zone around the mark equal to the height of the capital letter `D` on all four sides. No typography, icons, or borders may intrude into this zone.
2. **Minimum Sizes:**
   - Digital Header Wordmark: Never render below **24px** height.
   - Favicon Glyph: Minimum **16px** height.
   - Print Wordmark: Minimum **20mm** width.
3. **Strict Non-Negotiable Prohibitions:**
   - ❌ Never stretch, squish, or distort the aspect ratio.
   - ❌ Never apply dropshadows, outer glows, or bevel effects.
   - ❌ Never recolor the mark in rainbow or unapproved saturated tones.
   - ❌ Never place the dark wordmark over low-contrast dark gray backgrounds.
