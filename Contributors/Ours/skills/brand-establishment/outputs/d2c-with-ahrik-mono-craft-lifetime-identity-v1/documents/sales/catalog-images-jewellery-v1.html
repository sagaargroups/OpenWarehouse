The Luxury Friction

Precious Jewellery Photography Is A High-Stakes Nightmare

Insured courier logistics, armed studio security, dust particles, and flat studio reflections make fine jewellery shoots slow and dangerously expensive.

FRICTION 01
Vault & Transit Security
Transporting high-value diamond, gold, and gemstone inventories to commercial photo studios requires armed couriers, high insurance premiums, and constant theft anxiety.
FRICTION 02
Dust, Scratches & Polish Hell
Mirror-polished 18K gold reflects camera lenses, studio ceilings, and microscopic lint. Retouchers spend 20+ hours per ring smoothing surfaces, producing artificial plastic-looking results.
FRICTION 03
Gem Fire & Sparkle Blowout
Standard photography strobes either blow out diamond table facets with harsh white glares or leave emeralds and rubies looking dark, opaque, and lifeless.
