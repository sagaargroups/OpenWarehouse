## [Slide 02/07] — The Fashion Photoshoot Bottleneck
**Theme:** Mono-Craft (Canvas `#FFFFFF` Background, Surface `#F4F4F1` Cards, Obsidian `#111312` Text)  
**Layout:** 3-Column Problem Breakdown

### Header:
- **Category Badge:** THE FASHION FRICTION
- **Title (Gloock):** Fashion Photoshoots Are The Greatest Margin Drain
- **Lead Text:** Model casting agency fees, hair/makeup teams, and high e-commerce return rates due to inaccurate garment fit erode fashion profitability.

### Friction Columns:
1. **Friction 01: Casting & Model Fees**
   - Booking human models, makeup stylists, and studio space costs $15,000+ per collection. Re-shoots for late-arriving samples or sizing revisions double the expense.
2. **Friction 02: The 30%+ Sizing Return Crisis**
   - Static ghost mannequin shots fail to show how fabric moves, falls, and fits real human anatomy. Customers buy two sizes and return one, devastating profit margins.
3. **Friction 03: Colorway Explosion Overhead**
   - Shooting 8 color variants of a linen shirt traditionally requires 8 separate photo setups. Brands either blow their budget or show cheap digital color tints that look fake.
